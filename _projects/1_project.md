## Project Overview

This project aims to develop a state-of-the-art **AI-Driven Digital Twin Framework** to optimize resource utilization and enhance sustainability in tea cultivation. By integrating remote sensing, multispectral/hyperspectral imaging, and machine learning, the digital twin mimics the physical tea plantation environment, enabling real-time monitoring, predictive analytics, and decision support for farmers and agricultural managers.

### Project Details

- **Status**: Awarded

---

## Key Research Objectives

1. **Precision Agriculture Modeling**: Building digital twins of tea fields to model moisture dynamics, soil health, and crop growth.
2. **Moisture-Induced Spectral Analysis**: Applying machine learning algorithms on reflectance multispectral data to estimate soil moisture levels.
3. **Optimized Resource Allocation**: Simulating different irrigation and fertilization scenarios to maximize tea yield while minimizing environmental footprint.
