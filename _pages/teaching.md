<section class="teaching-page" aria-labelledby="teaching-title">
  <header class="teaching-page__header">
    <h2 id="teaching-title" class="visually-hidden">Teaching</h2>
    <p>
      Professor G. M. R. I. Godaliyadda teaches undergraduate and postgraduate subjects across signal processing,
      control systems, communication theory, stochastic modelling, and nonlinear dynamics. His teaching combines
      mathematical foundations with engineering analysis, system design, and modern signal-processing methods.
    </p>
  </header>

  <section class="teaching-page__section" aria-labelledby="undergraduate-title">
    <h2 id="undergraduate-title">Undergraduate Subjects</h2>
    <div class="teaching-page__divider" aria-hidden="true"></div>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE257: Signals and Systems</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Fundamentals of Signals &amp; Systems – Basic signal types, system properties, and continuous/discrete-time concepts.</li>
        <li>Fourier Series – Representing periodic signals as sums of sinusoidal components.</li>
        <li>Fourier Transform – Analyzing the frequency content of non-periodic signals.</li>
        <li>LTI System Analysis – Understanding system functions and the stability of Linear Time-Invariant (LTI) systems.</li>
        <li>Frequency Response – Studying how LTI systems respond to signals at different frequencies.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE352: Automatic Control</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Control System Fundamentals – Basic concepts, components, feedback, and control-system characteristics.</li>
        <li>System Modelling – Developing mathematical models and transfer functions for dynamic systems.</li>
        <li>Practical System Modelling – Modelling real-world mechanical, electrical, and other physical systems.</li>
        <li>Time &amp; Frequency Domain Analysis – Evaluating system performance, stability, and response in both domains.</li>
        <li>Continuous-Time Controller Design – Designing controllers to achieve desired stability and performance.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE353: Discrete Time Control Systems</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Discrete-Time Control Fundamentals – Core concepts, system representation, and stability of discrete-time control systems.</li>
        <li>Controller Discretization – Continuous-time controller approximations and conversion of analog controllers into digital form.</li>
        <li>Digital Controller Design – Discretization of control systems and direct design of digital controllers.</li>
        <li>State-Space Methods – State-space controller design and the use of state observers for system estimation.</li>
        <li>Practical Implementation Issues – Computational delay, numerical round-off, controller representation, and A/D and D/A quantization effects.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE401: Communication Theory</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Probability &amp; Random Variables – Fundamental probability concepts and statistical characterization of random variables.</li>
        <li>Random Processes – Analysis of time-varying random signals and their statistical properties.</li>
        <li>Gaussian Processes – Study of Gaussian random variables and processes commonly used in communication systems.</li>
        <li>Communication Systems in Noise – Evaluating communication-system performance and reliability in the presence of noise.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE539: Nonlinear and Multivariable Systems</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Nonlinear System Fundamentals – Core concepts and mathematical representation of nonlinear systems.</li>
        <li>Nonlinear System Stability – Analysis of stability, instability, and limit-cycle behavior.</li>
        <li>Nonlinear Control – Methods for controlling and improving the behavior of nonlinear systems.</li>
        <li>Multivariable System Analysis – Representation and performance analysis of multivariable systems.</li>
        <li>MIMO Control Systems – Introduction to control techniques for Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) systems.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE593: Advanced Signal Processing</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Signal &amp; Stochastic Processing Fundamentals – Core signal concepts and the basics of stochastic signal processing.</li>
        <li>Wiener Filtering – Optimal filtering techniques for noise reduction and signal estimation.</li>
        <li>Eigen Analysis &amp; Optimization – Performance surfaces, eigenvalue analysis, and iterative optimization methods.</li>
        <li>Adaptive &amp; Transform-Domain Processing – Adaptive signal processing algorithms and transform-domain approaches.</li>
        <li>Recent Signal Processing Advances – Emerging methods, applications, and developments in modern signal processing.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE2070: Random Signal Analysis</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Random Process Fundamentals – Continuous- and discrete-time random processes, ensembles, realizations, and finite-dimensional probability descriptions.</li>
        <li>Statistical Characterization – Mean, variance, autocorrelation, autocovariance, cross-correlation, and related ensemble statistics.</li>
        <li>Random Process Models &amp; Properties – Random walks, Wiener and Poisson processes, Markov processes, martingales, stationarity, and ergodicity.</li>
        <li>Spectral Analysis of Random Signals – Power spectral density, Wiener–Khinchin theorem, cross-PSD, and low-pass/band-pass random processes.</li>
        <li>Gaussian Processes &amp; Random Signals Through Systems – Gaussian-process modelling, LTI filtering of random signals, and noise concepts relevant to communication systems.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>
  </section>

  <section class="teaching-page__section" aria-labelledby="postgraduate-title">
    <h2 id="postgraduate-title">Postgraduate Subjects</h2>
    <div class="teaching-page__divider" aria-hidden="true"></div>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE 664: Random Signal Analysis for Communication and Signal Processing</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Probability &amp; Random Variables – Probability theory, individual random variables, and multiple random-variable concepts.</li>
        <li>Random Signals &amp; Statistics – Fundamental properties and statistical characterization of random signals.</li>
        <li>Random Signal Models – Mathematical models used to represent stochastic signals and processes.</li>
        <li>Random Signals Through Systems – Analysis of how random signals behave when transmitted through systems.</li>
        <li>Gaussian Processes – Study of Gaussian/normal processes and their applications in communication and signal processing.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE 698: Nonlinear Dynamics and Control</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Nonlinear Dynamics Fundamentals – Introduction to nonlinear systems and their dynamic behavior.</li>
        <li>Phase Plane Analysis – Graphical analysis of nonlinear system trajectories, equilibria, and stability.</li>
        <li>Lyapunov Stability Theory – Fundamental methods for assessing stability of nonlinear systems.</li>
        <li>Non-Autonomous Systems – Analysis of nonlinear systems whose dynamics vary with time.</li>
        <li>Nonlinear Control Design – Feedback linearization and other nonlinear control techniques.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="course-card">
      <h3>EE 686: Advanced Digital Signal Processing</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Discrete-Time Signals &amp; Systems – Fundamentals and time-domain analysis of discrete-time signals and systems.</li>
        <li>Z-Transform Methods – Use of the z-transform for analyzing and solving discrete-time system problems.</li>
        <li>Frequency-Domain Analysis – Analysis of signal and system behavior in the frequency domain.</li>
        <li>Stochastic Signal Processing – Study of random signals and systems, including Wiener filtering techniques.</li>
        <li>Advanced DSP Techniques – Selected advanced concepts and modern methods in digital signal processing.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>
  </section>
