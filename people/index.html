PhD Students and Alumni

Under the dedicated mentorship of Prof. Roshan Godaliyadda and his team of supervisors, our alumni have achieved remarkable global success. His rigorous research supervision and commitment to cultivating academic excellence have consistently empowered students to secure highly competitive PhD placements at the world's most prestigious institutions, including Princeton University, Columbia University, Cornell University, Rice University, University of Michigan, UIUC, UC San Diego, UCLA, and University of Waterloo.

Our past researchers have proudly advanced into leading doctoral programs and influential industry roles.
