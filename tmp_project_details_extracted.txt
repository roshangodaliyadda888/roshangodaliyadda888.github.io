MSI

both
 
reflectance
 
and
 
transmittance

This
 
modular

leverages
 
advanced
 
image
 
processing,
 
machine

and
 
deep
 
learning

robust
 
classification
 
and
 
quantitative

diverse

  Our  current  research  focuses  on  rapid,  non-invasive  agricultural  and  food  quality  applications.

USDA

HSI  

--- PAGE 17 ---
  Hyperspectral  imaging  captures  detailed  information  about  a  scene  across  hundreds  of  
narrow
 
and
 
contiguous
 
wavelength
 
bands,
 
providing
 
a
 
rich
 
spectral
 
signature
 
for
 
every
 
pixel.
 
Unlike
 
conventional
 
RGB
 
imagery,
 
this
 
allows
 
materials
 
with
 
similar
 
visual
 
appearances
 
to
 
be
 
distinguished
 
based
 
on
 
their
 
spectral
 
characteristics.
 
Hyperspectral
 
images
 
are
 
therefore
 
widely
 
used
 
in
 
applications
 
such
 
as
 
remote
 
sensing,
 
mineral
 
mapping,
 
precision
 
agriculture,
 
environmental
 
monitoring,
 
and
 
defence.
  A  major  challenge,  however,  is  that  the  limited  spatial  resolution  of  hyperspectral  sensors  
causes
 
many
 
pixels
 
to
 
contain
 
mixtures
 
of
 
several
 
materials.
 
Hyperspectral
 
Unmixing
 
(HSU)
 
aims
 
to
 
identify
 
these
 
constituent
 
materials,
 
known
 
as
 
endmembers
,
 
and
 
estimate
 
their
 
proportional
 
contributions,
 
or
 
abundances
,
 
within
 
each
 
pixel.
 
Our
 
project
 
investigates
 
HSU
 
with
 
particular
 
attention
 
to
 
spectral
 
variability
,
 
changes
 
in
 
material
 
signatures
 
caused
 
by
 
illumination,
 
atmospheric
 
conditions,
 
surface
 
topography,
 
shadows,
 
and
 
intrinsic
 
material
 
variations.
 
Understanding
 
and
 
compensating
 
for
 
these
 
variations
 
is
 
essential
 
for
 
developing
 
accurate
 
and
 
robust
 
hyperspectral
 
analysis
 
methods
 
for
 
real-world
 
environments.

Implicit  Neural  Representations  (INRs)  are  neural  networks  that  represent  signals  such  as  
images,

Our

and

applications.
 
A

is
 
spectral
 
bias
,

neural

to
 
learn
 
low-frequency
 
components
 
of
 
a
 
signal
 
faster
 
than
 
high-frequency
 
details.
 
Through
 
our
 
studies,
 
we
 
identified
 
that
 
odd
 
or
 
even
 
symmetry
 
in
 
activation
 
functions
 
contributes
 
to
 
this
 
behaviour.
 
To
 
address
 
this
 
limitation,
 
we
 
proposed
 
COSMO-INR,
 
a
 
new
 
INR
 
framework
 
that
 
achieved
 
state-of-the-art
 
performance
 
across
 
several
 
INR
 
benchmarks.
 
COSMO-INR
 
was
 
published
 
at
 
ICLR
 
2026
,
 
marking
 
the
 
first
 
top-tier
 
AI
 
conference
 
paper
 
produced
 
by
 
a
 
fully
 
Sri
 
Lankan-affiliated
 
research
 
team.
 
Beyond
 
theory,
 
we
 
apply
 
our
 
INR
 
advances
 
to
 
real-world
 
problems,
 
including
 
restoration
 
of
 
degraded
 
historical
 
images
 
for
 
cultural
 
heritage
 
preservation
 
through
 
the
 
Heritage
 
AI
 
project,
 
supported
 
by

Academic
 
Grant
.
